Our Story

From a Dream to Your Table

The Trench Restaurant was born out of a simple dream — to create a place where people could gather, share great food, and make lasting memories. What started as a small family kitchen in the heart of Nairobi has grown into one of the city's most beloved dining destinations.

Founded in 2026, we built our reputation on one promise — every dish that leaves our kitchen is made with the freshest ingredients, bold flavors, and genuine love for the craft of cooking.

Over the years we have served thousands of happy customers, hosted countless celebrations, and kept families coming back week after week. The Trench is more than a restaurant — it's a community.

How It Works

Use the simulator as a planning guide, then use the job and course pages to research real next steps.

1

Select your current career

Search and select the work you do now from the dropdown. It can be anything — teacher, cashier, nurse, designer, or support agent.

2

Select your target career

Search and select the career you want to move into. The app uses your selection for job searches and course suggestions.

3

Add your salary and time

Put in your current salary, weekly study hours, and how much transferable experience you already have.

4

Read the plan

The plan shows a salary estimate, possible skill gaps, a transition timeline specific to your pivot, and similar stories.

5

Open jobs and courses

Use the Jobs and Courses pages to open real searches based on the career you selected.

6

Treat it as a guide

The numbers are estimates — use them to compare paths and decide what to research more deeply.
